# Cinema & Teledrama Scheduler

A modern scheduling tool for managing film locations, scenes, and cast members.

## How to Use
1. **Open the Application**: Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in your web browser. No installation is required.
2. **Add Scenes**:
   - Enter Scene Number (e.g., 1, 1A).
   - Enter Location (English or Sinhala supported).
   - Select Day/Night.
   - Add Cast Members (type name and press Enter or click '+'). Max 10.
3. **Suggestions**: As you enter data, the app learns your locations and cast members and will suggest them next time you type.
4. **View Breakdowns**:
   - The **Master Schedule** shows all scenes in order.
   - The **Location Breakdowns** section automatically groups scenes by location.
5. **Download PDF**:
   - Click "Download Master PDF" for the full list.
   - Click "Download All Breakdowns" for a location-based report.

## Features
- 🌙 **Dark Mode Premium UI**: designed for comfortable viewing.
- 🇱🇰 **Language Support**: Full support for Sinhala and English text input.
- 💾 **Auto-Save**: Your data is saved automatically to your browser (Local Storage). You can close the window and come back later.
- 📊 **PDF Generation**: Instant reporting for production meetings.

## Note on PDF Support
The PDF generator uses standard fonts. For complex Sinhala scripts, the **On-Screen Display** is the reference. If the PDF does not render Sinhala characters correctly, you can also use your browser's **Print** function (Ctrl+P) and select "Save as PDF" for a perfect visual copy.
